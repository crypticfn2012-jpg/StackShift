Stack Shift
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This page describes what Stack Shift does with game-related information. Stack Shift is a browser puzzle game developed by Ron for PixelForge Studios.

Information collected by the game

Stack Shift does not ask you to enter your name, email address, phone number, password, or payment details. The game does not add its own advertising network or third-party analytics service.

Saved progress and settings

The game saves progress and preferences such as scores, achievements, selected cosmetics, sound settings, and language. On CrazyGames, the game uses the CrazyGames SDK data module when it is available; outside that environment, it uses browser storage on your device. Whether progress can be synced across devices depends on the hosting platform and its save features.

Browser storage can be removed through your browser’s site-data settings. Clearing it may delete local progress. Platform-managed cloud saves are controlled by the hosting platform.

Platform services and advertisements

When you play on CrazyGames, the platform may process information through its SDK, account, save, technical, or advertising services. Any rewarded advertisement in Stack Shift is requested through the CrazyGames SDK. Those services are governed by CrazyGames’ own privacy policy:

Read the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

Children and personal information

Stack Shift does not intentionally request personal information directly from players. Please follow the age requirements and privacy controls of the platform where you play.

Changes to this page

This policy may be updated as the game or its integrations change. The latest version will be available here.

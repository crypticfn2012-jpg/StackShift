Stack Shift
Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This page describes what Stack Shift does with game-related information. Stack Shift is a browser puzzle game developed by Ron for PixelForge Studios.

Information collected by the game

Stack Shift does not ask you to enter your name, email address, phone number, password, or payment details. The game does not add its own advertising network or third-party analytics service.

Saved progress and settings

The game saves progress and preferences such as scores, achievements, selected cosmetics, sound settings, language, and campaign progress. When running on CrazyGames, Stack Shift relies on the CrazyGames SDK Data Module for both guest and signed-in saves. When the game is hosted elsewhere, it uses browser storage on your device. Cross-device syncing depends on CrazyGames Data Module support and its Progress Save setting being enabled for the game.

Stack Shift does not operate its own account server and does not ask you to create a separate game password. Where CrazyGames account integration is available, you can use the platform’s own sign-in prompt. Guest progress is handled by the CrazyGames Data Module and can be synced to your CrazyGames account by the platform. Outside CrazyGames, clearing browser site data may delete local progress.

Platform services, leaderboards, and advertisements

When the CrazyGames leaderboard feature is enabled for Stack Shift, a signed-in player’s best score for the current weekly Timed Puzzle season may be submitted through the CrazyGames SDK. Daily Route, Endless, and Campaign scores are not submitted. CrazyGames processes account and leaderboard information under its own privacy policy. Stack Shift does not run a separate leaderboard server.

Any rewarded advertisement in Stack Shift is requested through the CrazyGames SDK. CrazyGames’ account, save, leaderboard, technical, and advertising services are governed by its privacy policy:

Read the CrazyGames Privacy Policy.

Children and personal information

Stack Shift does not intentionally request personal information directly from players. Please follow the age requirements and privacy controls of the platform where you play.

Changes to this page

This policy may be updated as the game or its integrations change. The latest version will be available here.
